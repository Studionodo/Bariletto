### Come si cura (EN)
Seventy-two hours invites leaving it sitting, and that's not a good idea: Seiko states the rate at full wind, and on a spent spring the movement performs worse. From stopped, twenty crown turns, which wind clockwise only. At the first click the crown sets the date and turns counter-clockwise only; at the second it stops the seconds and sets the time. Don't touch the date between nine at night and one in the morning. This isn't drawn by analogy from the 6R35: it's a line from Seiko's own 6R55 manual, and the stated risk is that the date fails to advance the following day. Service every two or three years.

### Cosa è normale
La scheda tecnica dichiara anche gli scarti che Seiko si concede oltre alla marcia: entro dieci secondi al giorno fra carica piena e giorno dopo, entro quindici fra una posizione e l'altra. Vuol dire che lo stesso orologio, appoggiato in due modi diversi sul comodino, può darti due numeri diversi senza avere nulla che non va.

### Cosa è normale (EN)
The spec sheet also states the margins Seiko allows beyond the rate itself: within ten seconds a day between full wind and the next day, within fifteen between one position and another. That means the same watch, set down two different ways on the nightstand, can give you two different numbers with nothing wrong at all.
